Information the App Handles

SMS Mass Sender is designed to process your data locally on your device. The app reads your local CSV files, extracts the phone numbers and custom details you provide, composes the text messages based on your templates, and sends them via your device's native SMS gateway. The app processes the following information:

1. CSV File Data

Your CSV files may contain information such as phone numbers, names, amounts due, or other custom details. You have full discretion over what data to include. Optional Testing: Users can test their templates by sending messages to specified test phone numbers. Users are advised to use appropriate test numbers during setup to prevent sending data to unintended recipients.

2. App Settings and Configuration

The app stores configuration data directly on your device, including folder profiles, SMS templates, categories, and test phone numbers. This information remains completely local to your device.

3. Purchase Information

If you purchase the paid feature unlock, the transaction is processed securely through Google Play Store Billing. SMS Mass Sender does not collect, access, or store your payment card numbers or billing details.

How Information Is Used

SMS Mass Sender uses the processed data strictly to provide the core functionalities of the app, including:

  • Creating, saving, and maintaining custom SMS Templates.
  • Automating bulk SMS sending via your device's carrier network to the numbers provided in your CSV file.
  • Loading and parsing your chosen CSV files.
  • Managing user-created local app settings and premium unlock statuses.

File Storage

Your CSV files are saved and accessed exclusively within the directories you select using Android's native system folder picker. The developer has no remote access to these folders.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Interaction

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your CSV files and message contents are never transmitted to the developer or any external third-party servers.

The app interacts with external services only when explicitly required for basic functionality:

  • Google Play Billing: To verify and process premium feature unlocks.
  • Android System File Picker: To securely locate, open, and manage files on your device.

If you choose to share your CSV files with other apps (such as cloud storage or email clients), that sharing is fully controlled by you and handled securely by the respective app you select.

Data Retention and Deletion

CSV files remain in your designated local folders until you choose to remove them. You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Manually deleting the files using your device's built-in file manager.
  • Deleting synced copies from your personal cloud storage (e.g., Google Drive).
  • Uninstalling the app, which automatically wipes all local configurations, templates, and app settings.

Note: Uninstalling the app will not automatically delete standalone CSV files stored in user-selected directories or manual backups on Google Drive; those must be removed manually.

Security

SMS Mass Sender relies on Android's secure storage access systems to safeguard your directories. Because the app does not use external servers to host your files, your data remains as secure as your physical device. You are responsible for maintaining the physical security of your device and securing your personal cloud accounts.

Children's Privacy

SMS Mass Sender does not intentionally collect or solicit data from children. The application is intended for general audiences who require bulk utility messaging features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
